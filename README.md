Privacy Policy


📄 Privacy Policy for Zentrox
Last Updated: May 8, 2026

1. Data Collection
Zentrox is designed as a music bot. To function, we collect and process the following data:

User IDs: To manage music queues and user-specific settings.

Server IDs: To store server-wide configurations (e.g., prefix, DJ roles).

Voice State Data: To connect to voice channels and play audio.

Usage Logs: Temporary logs of commands executed to improve bot performance and troubleshoot errors.

2. Data Usage
The data collected is used solely for the operation of the music playback service. We do not:

Sell your data to third parties.

Record or store audio conversations from voice channels.

Track user activity outside of commands directed at Zentrox.

3. Data Retention
User and server data are stored until the bot is removed from a server or until a request for data deletion is made. Temporary logs are purged every 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services
Zentrox utilizes external APIs (such as YouTube, Spotify, or SoundCloud) to fetch music. These services may collect their own data as per their respective privacy policies.

5. Contact & Deletion
Users may request the deletion of their data by contacting the developer via the GitHub repository issues or by removing the bot from their server.
